China Detains Protestant Pastor for ‘Illegal Religious Activities’ as Crackdown on Unregistered Churches Widens

Chinese authorities have detained Pastor An Yankui of the Taiyuan Xuncheng Reformed Church on suspicion of “organizing illegal religious activities,” according to the Christian Post and International Christian Concern. The detention is part of a widening crackdown on unregistered churches, with more than 150 pastors and church leaders still being held across China, according to Pastor Ezra Jin.

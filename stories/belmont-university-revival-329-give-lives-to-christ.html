329 Students Give Their Lives to Christ as Revival Night Closes Out UniteUS Season at Belmont

By GLM Omnimedia News November 13, 2026 Source: CBN News

Hundreds of students gave their lives to Jesus Christ on Tuesday night at Belmont University in Nashville, Tennessee, as a four-year spiritual awakening among America's young people continues to spread from campus to campus.

The night marked the final college outreach of 2026 for the UniteUS ministry. Thousands of students filled the Fisher Center for the Performing Arts for Unite Nashville, a night of worship and Gospel preaching that ended with 329 people making decisions for Christ, according to the ministry. Students were baptized outside the auditorium immediately afterward.

Worship was led by Circuit Riders, with messages preached by Tonya Prewett and Grant Troutt. In a post after the gathering, UniteUS wrote that for hundreds of students, the night they gave their lives to Jesus will forever mean that night in Nashville.

Students who were there shared their joy online. One Belmont student wrote, thanking the Lord Jesus for moving with power. Another supporter of the ministry called this generation something special, adding simply: Hungry. For. Jesus.

UniteUS also posted a prayer that the stories from Nashville would travel onward, into classrooms, through friend groups, and back home to families, so that people who were not even in the room might come to know Jesus because of what He did there.

The Belmont gathering caps a fall semester that has seen thousands of students turn to Christ at Pepperdine University, the University of Miami, Appalachian State University, and the University of South Alabama. Since the first Unite outreach was held at Auburn University on September 12, 2023, the movement has reached more than two dozen college campuses, with more than 100,000 students taking part.

What is happening on these campuses is not a program or a trend. It is young people hearing the Gospel plainly preached, responding in repentance and faith, and then publicly declaring that faith in baptism. That is the pattern of the early church, and it is still the pattern God uses today.

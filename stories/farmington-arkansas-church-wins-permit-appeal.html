Arkansas City Council Overturns Denial, Approves New Home for Real Church

By GLM Omnimedia News November 21, 2026 Source: The Christian Post

A small Arkansas congregation that was turned away by its city's planning commission has won its appeal. The Farmington City Council voted unanimously on November 9 to approve a conditional use permit for Real Church, clearing the way for the congregation to build a new facility on 12 acres off Arkansas Highway 170, according to reporting by The Christian Post.

The council's vote overturned an earlier decision by the Farmington Planning Commission, which had narrowly rejected the church's permit request on October 26 by a 4-3 vote. The church appealed that decision to the city council, which sided with the congregation.

Farmington City Attorney Jay Moore told The Christian Post that the council believed the location was appropriate for a church, noting that it sits close to the city's high school and a future junior high school. He added that the city recently completed major renovations to Highway 170 to handle heavier traffic flow on that road.

Moore said the planning commission had denied the permit because its members believed that part of the city should be reserved for two-acre, single-family zoning, and because they had questions at the time about whether the ground would perk properly for sewer placement.

The approval is not the end of the road for the church. According to Moore, Real Church must next present a large-scale development plan to the city for review by the city's engineer and other officials, and the timeframe for that step is up to the church. A Real Church spokesperson declined to comment when contacted by The Christian Post, but noted that the congregation "still need[s] approval on other things from [the] planning commission to move forward."

Real Church is a member congregation of the Assemblies of God, the world's largest Pentecostal denomination. On its website, the church describes its mission plainly: "This isn't about checking boxes or playing church — it's about real transformation. Whether you're brand new to faith or have followed Jesus for years, we're here to walk with you as you grow, heal, and step into the life God has for you."

Churches across America often face an uphill climb when they seek room to grow — zoning boards, traffic studies, and sewer questions can stall a congregation's plans for months or years. In Farmington, the elected council looked at the same facts the planning commission had weighed and reached a different conclusion: a church belongs in the life of a community, and a growing congregation deserves a place to gather. Real Church still has paperwork ahead of it, but its new home is one large step closer.

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

<p>"Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it" (Psalm 127:1). A church building is only bricks and timber unless God builds His Church within it — yet Scripture also commands believers not to forsake "the assembling of ourselves together" (Hebrews 10:25). When a congregation needs room to gather, pray, and grow, every open door is worth thanking God for. Pray for Real Church as it takes its next steps, and for churches everywhere facing zoning boards and permit hearings: that local officials would deal fairly, and that the Gospel would keep finding room to grow.</p>

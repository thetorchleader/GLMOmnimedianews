Detroit Lions’ Jack Campbell After Serious Neck Injury: ‘My Faith in God Has Never Been Stronger’

By GLM Omnimedia News November 5, 2026 Source: ChurchLeaders

Detroit Lions linebacker Jack Campbell says his faith in God has never been stronger as he recovers from a serious neck injury suffered on the field Sunday.

Campbell, 26, was carted off the field on Nov. 1 after being hurt while attempting to tackle Minnesota Vikings tight end T.J. Hockenson in the fourth quarter. The tackle left him face down on the field before medical professionals came to his aid, according to ChurchLeaders.

In a statement shared by the Detroit Lions on Tuesday, Campbell thanked fans for their prayers and gave an encouraging update on his recovery. “I’m incredibly encouraged by the progress I’ve made in such a short period of time,” he said. “I have full movement in my lower body, and I’m continuing to make progress with movement in my upper body.”

He was clear that the road ahead is not finished. “I know there’s still work ahead, but I remain very optimistic,” Campbell said. “I’m taking things one day at a time, staying positive, and focusing on getting better.”

At the heart of his statement was his faith. “My faith in God has never been stronger. I know He has a plan for me, and I trust in that plan,” Campbell said. “I know there may be challenges ahead, but with my faith, my family, and so many people in my corner, I know we’ll get through this.”

Campbell also thanked the athletic trainers and medical personnel who treated him on the field, the medical team at Henry Ford Hospital, and the Detroit Lions medical staff. “I especially want to thank the athletic trainers and medical personnel who responded and cared for me on the field,” he said. “The way you handled everything and looked after me when I needed it most is something I’ll never forget.”

He closed with a word about the city and team he plays for. “One thing I’ve learned about Detroit is that this city is built on toughness, resilience, and grit. That’s exactly how I’m going to approach this challenge,” Campbell said. “Please continue to keep me and my family in your prayers. We feel the love, and I can’t thank everyone enough. God is good. My faith is strong.”

Campbell’s words are a reminder that faith is not only for the good days. When the body is broken and the future is uncertain, trusting God’s plan is not a slogan — it is the anchor that holds.

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

Campbell’s response is Psalm 46:1 lived out before a watching nation: “God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.” When injury strips away strength, plans, and certainty, faith does not disappear — it becomes the very thing a man leans on, one day at a time.

74 Lashes for a Song: Iranian Singer Flogged for Performing Without a Hijab

By GLM Omnimedia News November 25, 2026 Source: Charisma News

Iranian singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four members of her musical team were each given 74 lashes, according to reports, for a concert video in which Ahmadi sang without the mandatory Islamic head covering. The punishment was reportedly carried out on Monday, November 23, in the city of Qom, nearly two years after the performance was first shared online.

The performance that drew the sentence was not a street protest and not a public rally. It was a musical video, filmed without a live audience, in which Ahmadi appeared in a sleeveless black dress and sang alongside male musicians. Iranian authorities convicted her and her associates of producing and publishing content deemed immoral and offensive to public decency.

In addition to the floggings, Ahmadi and the four musicians received two-year bans on any kind of artistic activity and on international travel. For a singer whose calling is her voice, the bans strike at her livelihood as surely as the lash strikes at her body.

The case has drawn condemnation beyond Iran's borders. United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Türk called the floggings “abhorrent,” and the punishment has renewed attention around the world on the cost borne by Iranian women who defy the country's dress laws.

It also recalls the death of Mahsa Amini, the 22-year-old woman who died in 2022 after being detained by Iran's morality police over an alleged hijab violation. Her death set off massive demonstrations across Iran, with women risking their lives to challenge the government's control over their clothing and their freedom.

Four years after Amini's death, the flogging of Ahmadi and her fellow musicians raises a hard question for the watching world: how much has really changed? The regime's answer, delivered in Qom, was 74 lashes for a song.

Christians who believe that every person bears the image of God cannot look away from such a punishment. A woman was beaten for singing. Her fellow musicians were beaten for playing. Whatever one thinks of the song, no song deserves the lash, and no government has the right to break a person's body for the crime of making music.

The Church is called to pray for the persecuted and the oppressed, and that call reaches beyond our own brothers and sisters in the faith. It reaches every person made in God's image who suffers under unjust power. Parastoo Ahmadi and her musicians need those prayers now, and so do the women of Iran who live every day under laws that punish them for the simple act of being seen and heard.

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

Every person is made in the image of God, and no one deserves to be beaten for singing. “Open thy mouth for the dumb in the cause of all such as are appointed to destruction. Open thy mouth, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy” (Proverbs 31:8–9). Pray for Parastoo Ahmadi, her fellow musicians, and the women of Iran.

Gary Sinise Says His Late Son's Faith in God Carried the Family Through Cancer's Darkest Days

By GLM Omnimedia News November 27, 2026 Source: ChurchLeaders

Oscar-nominated actor Gary Sinise says it was his late son's unshakable faith in God that carried their family through the darkest season of their lives. In a new interview with ChurchLeaders, Sinise spoke openly about his son McCanna "Mac" Sinise, whose courage in the face of a rare and deadly cancer strengthened the faith of everyone around him.

Sinise's new book, "Graceful Warrior: The True Story of a Son, a Father, and a Family Who Carried Each Other Through," was released on November 10, on what would have been Mac's 36th birthday. Mac was diagnosed in 2018 with chordoma, an extremely rare form of bone cancer that affects about 300 people in the United States each year. He died in early 2024, at the age of 33.

"He was just a tremendous person, very inspirational," Sinise told ChurchLeaders. "[He] just had such strong faith and courage, and he fought this with such grace."

Sinise began writing the book in the summer of 2024, hoping that sharing his family's experience would help others walking through similar suffering. The years of Mac's illness brought trial upon trial to the Sinise family. While Mac fought cancer, Sinise's father suffered a stroke, and Sinise's wife was diagnosed with cancer of her own.

The actor likened what his family endured to the famous scene in "Forrest Gump" in which his character, Lt. Dan, screams at God in the middle of a hurricane. "That's in some ways the way I felt during this raging storm that we're in," Sinise said.

Yet even in the storm, Sinise said, the family saw God's hand. "Amidst the chaos and the evil that we were fighting," he said, "there were many, many blessings and little miracles."

Watching his son's body weaken was the hardest part. Cancer slowly robbed Mac of his ability to play music, something he had loved since he was nine years old. But Mac's faith never wavered, and that steadfastness strengthened his father's own faith.

"He was hanging on to his faith. He knew that he was in God's hands," Sinise said. "The way he faced it was so courageous, and I knew his faith was so much a part of that, and that only strengthened ours."

Sinise acknowledged how easy it can be to ask why God allows such suffering. "Without faith and without Christ and without strength like that, how much harder would things be?" he asked. He said that neither he nor Mac ever felt angry at God for what was happening. Mac understood that suffering is a part of life, and that his circumstances, devastating as they were, were not unique.

"We kept our eyes on the things that were small miracles and small blessings throughout this evil chaos that we were going through," Sinise said. "We knew that we were surrounded by love. We had faith, and we had family, and we had that family to carry each other through."

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

Mac Sinise faced a fatal illness without anger toward God, trusting that he was in God's hands, and his faith carried his family. "The righteous cry, and the LORD heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles. The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart" (Psalm 34:17-18).

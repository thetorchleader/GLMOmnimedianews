Gunmen Attack Church Funeral in Jordan, Killing Seven and Wounding Dozens

By GLM Omnimedia News October 24, 2026 Source: Charisma News

KARAK, Jordan — Gunmen opened fire during a funeral at a Greek Orthodox church in southern Jordan on Friday, killing seven people and injuring 34, according to a later police update carried by Jordan's official Petra news agency.

The attack struck Hamoud Church in Karak governorate as mourners had gathered to bury a loved one. Jordan's Public Security Directorate initially reported six deaths and 28 injuries before Petra's breaking-news feed carried the higher figures, attributed to the directorate's chief.

Security camera footage published by the Saudi channel Al Arabiya showed three attackers at the scene. According to that footage, one carried an automatic weapon and two carried pistols, and one of the three appeared to suffer a weapon jam and never reached the church.

Security forces launched a search for the attackers. According to the directorate's account as reported by CBS News, the gunmen arrived in a vehicle and opened fire during the funeral procession at the church compound.

Jordan's King Abdullah II condemned the attack and pledged action against those responsible. "Every Jordanian life is precious, and we will strike with an iron fist against those who dare to threaten our nation," he wrote on social media, according to CBS News.

Authorities were working to identify the gunmen and establish the circumstances surrounding the shooting. "Investigations are underway in full swing, and we will update you with the results as they develop," a Public Security Directorate spokesperson said, according to Roya News.

Jordanian leaders described the shooting as terrorism, but the available official accounts had not established a specific motive or identified the attackers' affiliation. Karak is home to a longstanding Christian community in predominantly Muslim Jordan.

Christians in Jordan and around the world are being asked to pray for the wounded, the grieving families, and the congregation devastated by the attack. This is a developing story, and casualty figures and other details may change as authorities release more information.

Source: Charisma Magazine Online, reporting from Petra, CBS News, Roya News, and Al Arabiya.

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

A funeral is a place of grief and comfort, and violence there wounds a whole community twice over. Scripture calls the Church to weep with those who weep and to stand with the persecuted in prayer. "Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them; and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body" (Hebrews 13:3). Pray for the wounded, the grieving families, and the congregation in Karak — and for justice and peace in Jordan.

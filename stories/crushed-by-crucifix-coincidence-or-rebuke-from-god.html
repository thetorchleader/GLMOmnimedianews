Crushed by Crucifix: Is This a Coincidence or a Message of Rebuke From God to the Church?

By GLM Omnimedia News October 19, 2026 Source: Daniel Whyte III / Black Christian News

Horrifying moment priest is crushed to death by falling crucifix while live streaming Catholic Mass in Ecuador

Daniel Whyte III, President of Gospel Light Society International, says he does not believe this is an accident or a coincidence. Whyte believes, as he has been preaching for over 14 years, that God is not pleased with Evangelical pastors, Protestant pastors, Charismatic pastors, and Catholic pastors giving and receiving Communion unworthily with the Evangelical Files, the Catholic Files, and the Epstein Files full of child pornography, child trafficking, child molestation, and child rape raging before God's eyes, with most of these pastors silent at the cries of children when Jesus Christ said: "But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea." Whyte, who is known for predicting the coronavirus plague over a decade before it hit, in preaching and in writing, believes thousands of bishops, pastors, and priests were killed during the coronavirus plague because they were giving and taking Communion unworthily. "Wherefore whosoever shall eat this bread, and drink this cup of the Lord, unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord. But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of that bread, and drink of that cup. For he that eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh damnation to himself, not discerning the Lord's body. For this cause many are weak and sickly among you, and many sleep."

A Catholic priest in Ecuador was killed on Saturday, October 17, when a large wooden crucifix tore loose from the wall behind the altar and fell on him during a Mass he was streaming live. Father Patricio Alfredo Sánchez Molinari, 31, was leading the service at Our Lady of Perpetual Help Church in Guayaquil — about half an hour into the broadcast — when the heavy cross toppled onto him as he addressed his congregation. Worshippers can be heard on the footage screaming for an ambulance as others rushed forward to help. Emergency services were scrambled, but the priest was declared dead later that same day.

The Archdiocese of Guayaquil confirmed his death on Sunday, saying he had "departed for the Father's House," and sent condolences to his family and his Redemptorist community. Father Sánchez Molinari belonged to the Congregation of the Most Holy Redeemer and streamed his Masses regularly through his own social media accounts and a YouTube channel. Church officials have not said what caused the crucifix to fall.

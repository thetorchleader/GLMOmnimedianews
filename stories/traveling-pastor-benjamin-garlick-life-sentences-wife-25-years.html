Traveling Pastor Sentenced to Life in Prison for Abusing His Daughters; Wife Gets 25 Years

By GLM Omnimedia News November 15, 2026 Source: Protestia

A traveling pastor who ministered largely in the Hispanic community will spend the rest of his life in prison for sexually abusing his own three young daughters, according to reporting by Protestia.

Benjamin Garlick, 35, was found guilty in August of seven counts of aggravated rape of a child, six counts of aggravated sexual battery, solicitation of sexual exploitation of a minor, and two Child Protection Act violations. The court sentenced him to three life sentences plus 84 years in prison.

His wife, Shaantal Garlick, 32, was sentenced to 25 years in prison. She was convicted of two counts of facilitation of aggravated rape of a child and two counts of aggravated child neglect, after prosecutors showed that she knew the children were being abused and did nothing to stop it.

There were three victims in the case, girls between the ages of 5 and 8, all of them the couple's own daughters.

The sentences bring to a close a case that stands as a grievous warning to every church. A man who carried the title of pastor used a position of trust to commit terrible crimes against the most vulnerable people in his own home, and his wife, who should have been the children's protector, failed them as well.

When a church leader harms the innocent, the betrayal is twofold. It wounds the victims, and it wounds the witness of the church before a watching world. That is why the safety of children must never be treated as a secondary concern in any congregation.

Christians are called to pray for the healing of these three girls, to stand with them, and to make certain that no child in their care is ever left unprotected. Reporting suspected abuse to the authorities is not a betrayal of the church. It is obedience to God and love of neighbor, and it is the duty of every believer who sees a child in danger.

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

Scripture commands God's people to defend the fatherless: "Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy. Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked" (Psalm 82:3-4). A church that will not protect its children has forgotten the heart of God, and a just sentence, while it cannot undo the harm, affirms that the lives of the smallest among us matter to God and to the law.

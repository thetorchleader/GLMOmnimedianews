Daniel Whyte III says Michael Todd is not an apostle, nor is anybody else who is arrogant enough and ignorant enough to call themselves that

By GLM Omnimedia News November 19, 2026 Source: BCNN1

TULSA, Okla. (BCNN1) — Pastor Mike Todd of Transformation Church in Tulsa was anointed an “apostle” during a four-and-a-half-hour service on Sunday, according to Protestia, and is now to be known as “Apostle” Mike Todd.

Pastor Tim Ross led the affirmation during the service. With Todd kneeling before the congregation, Ross declared: “We the clergy and the people assembled support and confirm this Apostolic affirmation. Furthermore, we consent to the affirmation of Michael Alexander Todd to the office of Apostle. Beloved brother, you have been called to the sacred Office of Apostle and entrusted with the responsibility of leading God’s people as Apostolic overseer of Transformation Church.” Oil was then poured over Todd’s head, and the new title was made official.

The Bible knows nothing of men appointing themselves — or being appointed by other men — to the office of apostle in this day. The apostles were chosen by the Lord Jesus Christ Himself and were eyewitnesses of His resurrection. When the eleven replaced Judas, the man chosen had to be one who had “companied with us all the time that the Lord Jesus went in and out among us” and could be “a witness with us of his resurrection” (Acts 1:21–22). The church is “built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone” (Ephesians 2:20) — and a foundation is laid once, not again and again by every preacher who wants a bigger title. The apostle Paul, listing those who saw the risen Lord, put himself at the end of the line: “And last of all he was seen of me also, as of one born out of due time” (1 Corinthians 15:8).

Protestia, which reported the ceremony, has covered Todd before, including his praise of the late Bishop Carlton Pearson — a man who denied hell and embraced universalism — as a “man of God,” a sermon illustration in which Todd rubbed spit on a man’s face, and a message on what the Bible says about homosexuality that Protestia described as muddled and contradictory.

The title of apostle is not a promotion for popular preachers. It belonged to the men Christ called and sent in the first century, and no service, no oil, and no ceremony in Tulsa can make another one.

Source: Protestia, “Transformation Church ‘Pastor’ Mike Todd Anointed ‘APOSTLE’ Mike Todd,” November 16, 2026.

80,000 Fill AT&T Stadium for a Night of Prayer With 2819 Church

By GLM Omnimedia News October 13, 2026 Source: The Christian Post

An estimated 80,000 believers filled AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, on Saturday night for a gathering devoted to one thing: prayer. The event, called “ACCESS — That Powerful Hour of Prayer,” was led by Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell and his 2819 Church, and free tickets were claimed in just eight hours, according to reporting by The Christian Post.

There were no celebrity speakers on the stage, no official sermon, and none of the trappings of a standard concert. Instead, the evening centered on what the church calls “The Cry” — a unified corporate prayer — and the simple, ancient declaration: “Maranatha! Come, Lord Jesus.” Attendees spent a full hour in prayer and another 45 minutes in worship, lifting up their lives, families, cities, nation, and the nations of the world.

Several well-known pastors were among the crowd. Ed Newton, senior pastor of Community Bible Church in San Antonio, said the night carried a tangible sense of God’s presence. Writing on social media after the event, he described a moment in which it felt as though God “walked in the room and spoke right to you,” and he honored Mitchell and 2819 Church for a night of “pleading, warring, contending for our lives, families, cities, nation, and the nations to turn to Christ.”

Josh Howerton, senior pastor of the multisite Lakepointe Church in Texas, said he was “grateful to have witnessed” the gathering, describing 80,000 people “storming heaven in prayer” and praising the move of God he saw through Mitchell and the church.

The 2819 Access Tour is an annual event, and its growth has been striking. Last year’s ACCESS gathering in Atlanta drew more than 30,000 people to State Farm Arena — a number more than doubled this year in the much larger AT&T Stadium.

Malachi O’Brien, senior pastor of The Church at Pleasant Ridge in Kansas City, Missouri, offered perspective on what the crowds may not see. He wrote that Mitchell’s public fruit grew from private sacrifice: years of behind-the-scenes ministry, and a prayer meeting that began in 2013 with just twelve desperate people crying out to God. “God rewards in public what He alone sees in secret,” O’Brien said, urging believers not to despise small beginnings.

The event follows a recent social media debate over Mitchell’s ticketed “Street Preachers” tour with Pastor Tim Timberlake, which included VIP packages priced at $200. In fairness, Saturday’s ACCESS tickets were free, and the church did not immediately respond to The Christian Post’s request for comment on the broader discussion.

The gathering also coincided with the release of Mitchell’s new book, Contend: Standing Boldly for Biblical Truth While Winning the War Within.

Whatever the long-term fruit may be, tens of thousands of Christians chose to spend a Saturday night in October doing what the early church did when it faced its hardest hours: praying together. In a nation hungry for renewal, 80,000 voices crying out to God in one stadium is a sound worth hearing.

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

“If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land” (2 Chronicles 7:14). The early church gathered for prayer when it needed God most, and Acts records that “when they had prayed, the place was shaken where they were assembled together” (Acts 4:31). May this gathering in Texas spark a lasting return to earnest, united prayer in churches everywhere.

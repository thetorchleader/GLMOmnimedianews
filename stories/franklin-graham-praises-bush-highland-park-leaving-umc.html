Franklin Graham Praises Bush and Highland Park Church for Standing on the Word of God

By GLM Omnimedia News November 19, 2026 Source: Charisma News

Evangelist Franklin Graham is commending former President George W. Bush and Highland Park Methodist Church in Dallas for standing firm on the Word of God after the congregation voted to leave the United Methodist Church.

In a post on X on November 16, Graham applauded the church's decision to withdraw from the denomination after 110 years, pointing to the United Methodist Church's embrace of LGBTQ clergy and same-sex marriage.

“They chose to stand up against the unbiblical progressive movement that has hijacked the United Methodist denomination,” Graham wrote.

Graham also thanked the Bushes, who have long worshipped at Highland Park, for supporting the congregation's decision. “Thank you Highland Park Methodist Church and President Bush for doing the right thing and standing with the Word of God,” he wrote.

The 15,000-member congregation's break with the denomination has been building for some time. According to Charisma News, the dispute escalated after the denomination's Horizon Texas Conference filed a lawsuit challenging changes to the congregation's governing documents. The legal battle is continuing, and no final outcome has been reported.

Following the congregation's overwhelming vote in October, nearly 8,000 people gathered for worship at Highland Park as the church moved forward with its separation, despite continued opposition from denominational leadership.

The Houston Chronicle, citing The New York Times, reported that the Bushes were among those who supported the congregation's departure.

The United Methodist Church removed its longstanding restrictions on same-sex marriage and LGBTQ clergy in 2024, after years of controversy over biblical authority, marriage, and sexuality.

For Graham, the lesson is plain: standing on God's Word is worth the fight, whatever the cost.

Biblical Worldview Takeaway

When a church must choose between the approval of a denomination and the authority of Scripture, the Word of God must win. “Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path” (Psalm 119:105, KJV). Faithfulness is not measured by how many stay, but by whether we stand where God has told us to stand.
